Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Your email address — used only for sign-in via magic link. Never shared.
  • Information you enter: your name, baby nickname, partner name, GC name, due date, journal entries, tasks, budget, and shopping data.
  • Anonymous usage events (card track and phase read) via Plausible Analytics — cookieless, no personal data collected.

How it's stored

Your data is stored in your browser's localStorage and in Supabase (encrypted at rest). Row Level Security ensures only your authenticated session can read or modify your data. No one else can access it.

What we do — and don't do — with your data

  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not read your journal or personal entries.
  • Your data is yours. Export it any time from Settings → Backup & restore.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Danger zone → "Delete my account & all data" to permanently remove your cloud data and sign out. Or email granthansell@gmail.com.

Analytics

Plausible Analytics logs which cards you read (track and phase only — no personal identifiers). No cookies. No cross-site tracking.
